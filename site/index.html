          How to play
          
  
            
    
          1. Each day there are five places, the same for everyone (the day changes at 00:00 UTC).
            2. 
    
          2. Point at each one in a straight line through the Earth. Nearby places are just below the horizon; the other side of the world is nearly straight down.
            3. 
    
          3. Your score per place is out of 100: full marks within 5°, falling to zero at 90° off. 500 is perfect.
            4. 
    
          4. After each guess, the radar shows the true direction (the map is centred on you, with every place in its true compass direction) and the bar shows how far down it is. Sweep around to find it.
            5. 
  
          
  
          Modes
          
  
            
    
          • Point your phone uses the compass and tilt sensors. Choose to aim the back camera or the top edge.
            • 
    
          • Map lets you turn a 3D arrow on a satellite view. Works on a computer too.
            • 
    
          • Fixed spot: don't want to share your location with the game? Everyone points from the same city, which changes daily.
            • 
  
          
  
          Each mode has its own leaderboard. Weekdays get harder: Monday is famous capitals, Saturday smaller cities and landmarks, Sunday a mix.
