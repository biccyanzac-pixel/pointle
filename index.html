      How to play
      
                
        
              1. Three places a day, the same for everyone. The day changes at 00:00 UTC.
                2. 
        
              2. Point at each one in a straight line through the Earth. Nearby places are just below the horizon; the other side of the world is nearly straight down.
                3. 
        
              3. 100 points per place: full marks within 5°, falling to zero at 90° off.
                4. 
        
              4. After each guess, the radar shows the true direction (a map centred on you, with every place in its true compass direction), the bar shows how far down it is, and the globe shows the tunnel. Sweep around to find it.
                5. 
        
              5. Modes, each with its own leaderboard: point your phone (compass and tilt sensors), play on the map (drag a 3D arrow), or use today's fixed spot if you'd rather not share your location with the game.
                6. 
        
              6. Weekdays get harder: Monday is famous capitals, Saturday smaller cities and landmarks, Sunday a mix.
